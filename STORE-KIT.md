## 1. Store promise

**Understand any Indian company in 60 seconds — and keep what you learn.**

## 2. Customer and buying / use moment

- **Primary customer:** Indian MBA students and aspirants preparing for placements, interviews and group discussions.
- **Secondary customer:** Graduate students and early-career professionals moving into finance, consulting or strategy.
- **Context:** Busy, mostly on their phones, need to know many companies quickly.
- **The moment:** Placement season or an interview is days away. They have a list of company names but no time. Annual reports, news and YouTube are too slow and are forgotten within a week. When asked "How does Zepto make money?" or "What's happening in quick commerce?", they are unprepared.
- **What ANANT.AI does in that moment:** They search the company, watch a 60-second explainer, keep a 30-word note, and revise their notebook before the interview.
- **The small change they want:** Walk into the interview able to explain a company clearly, with numbers they can trust.

## 3. Commercial position and visual direction

- **Commercial position:** Premium and restrained. Free entry with an attainable Pro tier (₹149/month).
- **Chosen direction (locked):** **The Sixty.** Black-room focus with one minute and full attention. A dark, cinematic, vertical explainer that ends on a beige "kept" screen. Speed and concentration are the brand, and the reward is ownership of what you learned.
- **Emotional signal:** Fast and calm, like a smart friend briefing you before an interview.

## 6. Visual rules (observable)

### Typography
- Display: **Inter Tight Semibold** (fallback Geist) for part titles, company names and key figures. Key figures are 64–88 px on mobile.
- Accent: **Instrument Serif Italic**, used for **one** phrase per screen at most.
- Body: Inter Regular, 16–18 px, at most 3 lines per part.
- Labels: uppercase, letter-spaced +8%, Ash: `SELLS · EARNS · SIZE · RIVALS · WATCH`.
- Source lines: Inter 12 px, Ash, directly under every figure, e.g. "Source: FY25 Annual Report".

### Lighting
- Low-key spotlight. Bone type appears out of Black Room, as if lit from above.
- The kept screens (note, quiz, notebook) switch to flat, even Paper light.
- No photography, no stock imagery, no company logos.

### Composition
- One part per full screen. Five parts plus one note screen form one explainer.
- A thin Brass progress line runs across the top and counts down 60 seconds.
- One idea, one figure and one source per screen. At least 40% of each screen is empty space.
- Dark is for watching and Paper is for keeping. The switch from dark to light marks the reward.

### Materials (interface)
- Matte flat surfaces, no shadows on dark screens, hairline (1 px) Ash dividers.
- Buttons: full-width, 52 px tall, square-cornered (4 px radius). Bone on Black Room, or Black Room on Paper.
- No glassmorphism, gradients, glows or badges.

### Motion
- Explainer: kinetic type cuts in at about 150 ms, each part holds for about 10 s, and the Brass line runs continuously.
- Reward: a slow 600 ms fade from Black Room to Paper, then the note slides up and settles.
- Streak: a single Brass mark fills in. No confetti, bounce or sound effects.

### Voice
- Short and punchy, with second-person sentences under 12 words.
- Plain English and no jargon. When a term is necessary, define it in five words.
- Never use buy, sell, target, invest, returns, rally or crash.

## 8. Non-negotiables

1. Every figure shows its source and period on the same screen. If there is no source, the figure is not shown.
2. Every explainer has exactly five parts: sells, earns, size, rivals, watch.
3. Every explainer ends with something kept: a 30-word note and a quiz.
4. Only five colours, and Brass is the only accent.
5. Dark is for watching and Paper is for keeping, with no exceptions.
6. The educational-only disclaimer appears on the site and inside the app.
7. The three products, their names and the ₹149/month Pro price are fixed.

## 9. Avoid

- Stock prices, price charts, candlesticks, tickers or market-cap movement indicators
- Red and green colour coding, or up/down arrows next to numbers
- Buy, sell, hold, target price, "multibagger", or any return or wealth language
- "Get rich", "beat the market", "guaranteed", or clickbait phrasing
- Dense tables, long reports, or more than three lines of body text per screen
- Confetti, coins, leaderboards, levels, loot boxes or other game-style gamification
- Company logos, stock photos of offices or traders, and glowing fintech dashboards
- Unsourced numbers, estimates presented as facts, or "every company" coverage claims until coverage is complete
- Claims of SEBI registration, certification accreditation, placement or job outcomes, or user counts
